# Section 6 limitations and readiness

**SECTION 6 NOT YET SUPPORTED** for the full requested domain. A provisional Section 6 narrative and reproducible bounded execution evidence are supplied.

1. The implementation is a headless semantic model. Browser reflow, actual viewport behaviour, DOM reachability and real IME/selection events were not measured.
2. Several C→N conditions and action guards are supplied as truth-valued semantic witnesses. Their gating is tested; complete evidence acquisition and predicate derivation are not.
3. The 24 action families have gate/registry coverage, not complete postcondition, content-equivalence, property-effect and per-scope coverage. A configured service validator exists, but full workflow and validator-boundary execution is incomplete.
4. All sixteen rule masks are represented by filter projections. Fully justified, independent scoped-need scenarios for every combination remain required.
5. The canonical tie key is restricted to simple NFC strings. Full typed tuple, exact numeric, sequence and declared-set normalisation remains unimplemented.
6. Invariant metrics concern local model predicates at recorded admission boundaries and negative proposed-mutation controls. They do not establish complete service-integrity enforcement or actual UI preservation.
7. Transaction feedback is a machine-readable baseline service output. It is not evidence of a rendered status panel or full adaptive R3.A5 coverage. The deterministic single-process ledger does not establish distributed backend guarantees.
8. Trace acceptance checks required structure, fields, parent links and selected supersession behaviour. It cannot independently prove the truth of every injected witness or every semantic statement in the log.
9. Implementation and oracle translation were produced in the same work session. Expectations were frozen separately and never obtained by querying the implementation, but independent external review has not occurred.
10. The 775-entry inventory is not exhaustive. Five gap entries describe categories of unfinished work, not a finite count of five remaining tests. At least one case per 35 parent/44 current family identifier does not imply complete coverage.
11. The final selected dataset contains 770 matching executions. Earlier eight trace and five transaction acceptance failures remain recorded. Three implementation/harness defects were corrected and retested; one known canonical-domain limitation and broader coverage gaps remain open. No specification contradiction was discovered.
12. There were no human participants or UX, comprehension, cognitive-load, usability or clinical-effectiveness measurements.

No results figure is supplied: a pass-percentage chart would obscure the more important differences between gate/registry checks, behavioural cases, independent acceptance audits and unexecuted scope. Tables 10–14 provide the appropriate detail.

Sections 1–5 have not been edited, and Section 7 has not been drafted. Further work should close the implementation and coverage gaps rather than reinterpret the existing passing subset as complete verification.
