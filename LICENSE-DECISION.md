# License decision

This repository uses a split licensing model.

## Software and source code

Source code and software materials in this repository are released under the
MIT License, unless a specific file or subdirectory states otherwise.

See `LICENSE` for the full MIT License text.

## Research materials

Non-software research materials are released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0), unless a specific file or
subdirectory states otherwise.

This includes research specifications, documentation, synthetic research data,
verification materials, reproducibility materials, and supporting research
documentation.

See `LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.md` for the applicable license terms.

## Third-party materials

Third-party materials, if any, remain subject to their original licenses,
copyrights, permissions, and other applicable terms. The repository licenses
do not grant rights to third-party materials that are not owned by the authors.

## Publication

The licensing of this repository is separate from the publication license of
any manuscript subsequently published using these materials.

If an article is accepted by the Journal on Interactive Systems (JIS), the
published article will be subject to the journal's stated Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) publication terms.

## Archival release

Before creating a versioned archival release or DOI record, the authors will
verify that repository contents are appropriately licensed and that all
necessary third-party permissions have been obtained.
